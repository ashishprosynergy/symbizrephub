How to use Rep Hub

Rep Hub is Symbiz's tool for looking after Growth Rings: who to call, when rings meet, visits, events and summits, and hours and expenses. It works on a phone, a tablet or a computer, and everything comes from Zoho.

For repsFor Mel & the office

For reps

Your rings, your calls and your events, in one place.

Getting started

  1. Open the email "… has invited you to Rep Hub" and tap Choose my password. No email? Go to the sign-in page and tap Invited? Email me a link to set up my account.
  2. Sign in with your email and the password you chose.
  3. Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen; Chrome → ⋮ → Add to Home screen.
☀️

Today

Who is due a call, soonest first.

  • Tap a name to phone them.
  • Tap Log after the call — Rep Hub books the next one for you.
  • No answer? Log that too; it tries again in a few days.
◎

My Rings

Every Growth Ring you look after.

  • Members, facilitator and when they meet.
  • Record a ring meeting and who came.
  • Start a new ring; the office approves it.
👥

People

Your members and facilitators.

  • Phone, email and address in one tap.
  • When you last spoke, and renewals coming up.
  • Every member on the Member map.
💵

A/R over 60 days

Your members with an invoice more than 60 days past due in Zoho Books.

  • Call each one to find out where their membership stands.
  • Tap Log a call afterwards — the list then shows them as contacted.
  • Also shown at the top of Today.
📅

Calendar

Ring meetings, your visits, events and summits — in one month view.

  • Tap a day to see what's on.
  • Tap a place to open it in Maps.
🚩

Events & summits

Regional and Global Summits and info meetings.

  • Register a member: pick one of your members and it fills in their details.
  • See who's coming.
  • Follow each registration: waiting, in Zoho, or sent back.
📍

Visits

Sitting in with each ring once or twice a year.

  • In Calendar, tap Schedule a visit and search the place by name.
  • Afterwards, file the short visit report.
🧾

Hours & expenses

Paid for what you do.

  • Log hours, or start the timer.
  • Add an expense and photograph the receipt.
  • Sent back? Read why, then Fix and send again.
🔔

Notifications

The bell tells you what needs you: calls due, renewals, visits, events coming up, and anything sent back.

No signal? Keep working. Calls, hours and expenses you save are kept on your phone or tablet, and sent on their own when you have signal again — a bar at the top shows how many are waiting. A very large receipt photo may have to wait until you have signal; Rep Hub tells you if so.

For Mel & the office

How the network is doing, how each rep is doing, and what needs you.

▦

Overview

The network at a glance.

  • Members, rings, retention and who joined.
  • Rings at risk, and why.
  • Summit planning: how far each summit's checklist has got.
💼

Rep manager

How each rep is doing.

  • Needs your attention: reps gone quiet, late facilitator calls, rings not meeting, check-ins due.
  • Sort by what matters; open any number for the detail.
  • See their screen exactly as the rep sees it.
🎯

Goals & check-ins

  • Set quarterly goals for calls, visits and new members; reps see their progress.
  • Keep private check-in notes with a next check-in date.
✉️

Reps

  • Invite reps: a welcome email with a link to choose a password.
  • See who has signed in, and send again.
  • Message reps: one email to all or some.
  • Move rings between reps; Zoho is updated.
✅

Approvals

Nothing reaches Zoho until you say so.

  • Hours and expenses: Approve or Decline with a reason.
  • Registrations and leads from reps — and from QR sign-ups.
  • New Growth Rings.
🚩

Summits

  • Planning checklist for each summit; pick Who does each task — they're emailed.
  • Edit Symbiz's standard checklist.
  • Registration QR: print it on a poster or slide; people register themselves.
  • Notes for next time and past summits' numbers.
🗺️

Member map & visits

  • Every member on a map, by level or by rep.
  • Growth Rings layer, and the nearest rings for members not in one.
  • Visit plan: rings not visited in 6 months; book a visit for a rep.
📈

Reports

  • Retention, who joined and who left.
  • Coverage by rep; export to CSV or PDF.
  • The Monday email sums up each rep's week.
💵

A/R over 60 days

From the unpaid invoices in Zoho Books, grouped by rep.

  • See who each rep has contacted since the invoice went 60 days over, and who is still waiting.
  • Email this rep to nudge them.
💾

Backup

Hours, expenses, approvals and summit checklists are kept in Rep Hub; calls, registrations and leads in Zoho.

  • Every Sunday a copy is emailed to the owner.
  • Access & security → Backup: email a copy now, or download one with the receipt files.
Registering people with a QR code: open a summit or event and press Registration QR. Print the poster or show it on screen. Each sign-up arrives on Approvals; approve it and it goes into Zoho.
